### Focus-Tracker Workflow

1. 2-clicks before starting a focused session - i. open [application —→](https://abhishek-pomodoro.streamlit.app/) ; ii. click start session
2. Forget about this tracker and give complete focus to work-at-hand
3. When the session ends, a form opens to log work. I intend to track, how complex the work was, whether I was able to focus or not,
and how productive the session went.
4. I also collect additional notes to track (and possibly come back to) to analyse at the end of month.
5. The form does API call to google sheet and logs the details.
